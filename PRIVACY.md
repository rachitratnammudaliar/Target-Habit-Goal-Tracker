# Privacy policy

**Target – Habit & Goal Tracker** · Last updated 3 November 2026

Target is made by Rachit Ratnam Mudaliar. It's designed so that nothing about you leaves your phone.

- **No internet.** The app has no permission to use the internet, so it can't send anything anywhere.
- **No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.**
- **Your targets and history** are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. On Android they're excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.
- **Health data (Android).** If you connect Samsung Health, Target reads steps, distance, exercise sessions and active calories from Health Connect on your phone, only to fill in the targets you've linked to it. It never writes health data, never stores more than each day's total for those targets, and never shares it. You can remove access at any time in **Settings → Health Connect**.
- **Backups** are files you create and keep yourself.
