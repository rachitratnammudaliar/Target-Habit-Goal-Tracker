# Changelog

## 1.2.0

Move more, study more, and see how the week went.

- **Move target:** walking, running and steps count together, 10 km or 13,500 steps, whichever you're further along on. Samsung Health fills it in on Android
- **Levels, not just done or missed:** 60% is partly achieved, 80% satisfied, 100% done, 130% overachieved ⭐. Satisfied days count as a hit, and partly achieved days keep your streak alive. Teal on the heatmap and charts marks an overachieved day
- **Hours targets:** log how many hours you did (30 min, 1 h, 2 h or any amount like 1:30), for self-study, coding or reading
- **Total for the week:** 15 hours of study in any amounts on any days, with the same levels
- **Weekly and monthly summary** on Progress: targets hit against the period before, totals (km, steps, hours), overachieved count, best and toughest day, most consistent target and the one that needs attention. Browse earlier weeks and months, and Share it as text
- **Fixed:** "In bed by 23:45" logged by hand as 02:00 counted as early for the next day. It is now after midnight, late, and counts for last night
- Changed: a day with under 60% of an amount target is now Missed (it used to be "not quite" with any progress)

## 1.1.0

Targets that fit a real timetable.

- **Any time:** a "done or not" target has no time at all. Tick it whenever you do it
- **Times a week:** run 3 times a week, gym 4 times a week, on whichever days suit you. Not going on a given day is never "missed"; the streak counts weeks
- **A different time on some days:** library by 10:00 on Tuesday and 13:00 on Thursday
- **Change just today's time:** a class moved? ⋮ → Change today's target time. Your usual time stays
- **Pause** a target for 3 days, a week, a month or until a date (holidays, exams, ill). Paused days never break a streak
- **Day notes:** "exam week", "new route", shown on Today and in the history
- **Clear old history:** older than 3, 6 or 12 months, everything, or one target's history
- **Monthly report** on Progress: targets hit this month, longest streak, most improved
- **Android widget:** today's targets on the home screen with a one-tap ✓
- **Fixed:** changing a target's time no longer rewrites past days; each day keeps the time it had
- **Fixed:** "In bed by 23:00" logged at 00:30 now counts for last night, as late, not for the new day
- **Fixed:** a missed time target now shows **Overdue** once its time has passed
- **Fixed:** you can set the day a target starts, so earlier days don't count against you
- **Fixed:** unticking a "done or not" target has Undo too

## 1.0.0

The first version of Target, for Android and iPhone.

- **Targets, not to-dos:** wake up by 7:00, at the library by 9:00, run 5 km, 10,000 steps, gym on Mondays and Wednesdays
- **Three kinds:** done or not, by a time of day, or an amount (km, minutes, steps, kcal, pages, glasses…)
- **Samsung Health (Android):** steps, running or walking distance, active time and active calories tick your targets by themselves, through Health Connect on your phone
- **Today:** a ring with how many targets you've hit, one tap to log, streaks on every target, and ‹ › to fill in earlier days
- **Progress:** hit rate for 7, 30 or 90 days, a bar for every day, and a calendar heatmap for each target
- **Each target:** current and best streak, 30-day hit rate, a 17-week heatmap, and a chart of your times or amounts against the target
- **Rest days and skips** never break a streak
- **Backup** to a file that Android and iPhone both read
- Works offline: no internet permission, no account, no ads, no tracking
