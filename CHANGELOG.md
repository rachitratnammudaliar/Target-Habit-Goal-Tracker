# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first version of Target, for Android and iPhone.

- **Targets, not to-dos:** wake up by 7:00, at the library by 9:00, run 5 km, 10,000 steps, gym on Mondays and Wednesdays
- **Three kinds:** done or not, by a time of day, or an amount (km, minutes, steps, kcal, pages, glasses…)
- **Samsung Health (Android):** steps, running or walking distance, active time and active calories tick your targets by themselves, through Health Connect on your phone
- **Today:** a ring with how many targets you've hit, one tap to log, streaks on every target, and ‹ › to fill in earlier days
- **Progress:** hit rate for 7, 30 or 90 days, a bar for every day, and a calendar heatmap for each target
- **Each target:** current and best streak, 30-day hit rate, a 17-week heatmap, and a chart of your times or amounts against the target
- **Rest days and skips** never break a streak
- **Backup** to a file that Android and iPhone both read
- Works offline: no internet permission, no account, no ads, no tracking
